The Problem

You aren't getting hacked. You're leaving doors open.

Most wallet losses don't begin with a brute-force hack. They begin with forgotten approvals, risky signatures, hidden permissions, and exposure users never mapped.

  • Invisible Permissions: forgotten approvals can leave assets exposed long after a transaction is complete.
  • No Real-Time Awareness: most wallets don't tell users when risk is building.
  • Unknown Blast Radius: users rarely know how much a malicious spender can actually reach.

Elofid Firewall was built to close these gaps before they become losses.

Reward Eligibility Rules

A few simple rules decide which CYCX earns a reward in each cycle. Please read them before you continue.

  1. Keep CYCX in your own Web3 wallet for 60 full days.
    MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Ledger and similar self-custody wallets qualify. CYCX held on an exchange — Binance, Coinbase, KuCoin or any other — does NOT count, because those tokens are not in your wallet.
  2. Every amount you receive starts its own 60-day count.
    New CYCX begins counting from the moment it arrives in your wallet. Older CYCX keeps the age it already earned.
  3. If you move part of your CYCX out, the oldest portion is counted as leaving first.
    This is a FIFO calculation used for eligibility only. The CYCX that remains keeps its recorded holding age — a partial sale or transfer does not reset the rest of your balance.
  4. Your eligible balance is fixed at the cycle snapshot.
    Only CYCX that completed the full 60 days by the moment of the snapshot can earn a reward in that cycle.
  5. Rewards must be claimed for each cycle.
    Unclaimed rewards do not roll over and are not added to the next cycle. Each reward cycle is calculated separately.
  6. Only the wallet that held the CYCX can claim.
    Each allocation is secured by a Merkle proof, and the smart contract only allows that wallet to claim its own allocation.
  7. Claimed rewards can qualify in future cycles.
    Once claimed, they sit in your wallet like any other CYCX and start their own 60-day count.
The reward rate is a per-cycle target calculated off-chain, not a fixed or guaranteed return.

By continuing, you confirm that you have read and understood how Elofid reward eligibility is calculated.
