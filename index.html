Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. Scans read public blockchain data only. No wallet connection, signature or seed phrase is needed to scan. You connect a wallet only if you choose to revoke an approval.

Is it free?

Yes. Scanning in the Security Hub, the Firewall and the Scanner Bot is free. The Developer API has a Free plan and paid plans with higher limits and longer history.

Which networks does Elofid support?

Token and contract scans cover 12 networks: BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche, Linea, Scroll, opBNB, Robinhood Chain and Solana. Wallet approval scans in the Firewall cover BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum and Polygon.

What is Exit Depth?

How much of a token can actually be sold within 10% price impact. It is shown only when Elofid measures it exactly. Otherwise it shows a dash, never an estimate.

Is an Elofid scan a security audit?

No. Scores, signals and warnings are informational. They are not an audit, financial advice or a guarantee of safety. Scans reduce risk but cannot eliminate it.
